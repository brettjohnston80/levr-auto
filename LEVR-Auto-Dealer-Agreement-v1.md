# LEVR Auto — Dealer Agreement (Working Draft v1)

*Working draft — not legal advice, not ready to send to a real dealer until an attorney has reviewed it, especially Sections 1 and 8 (relationship/compensation language), which depend on the still-pending state licensing research.*

---

## 1. Purpose & Relationship

LEVR Auto represents verified, ready-to-buy customers and requests pricing on their behalf. LEVR Auto is not purchasing the vehicle, is not a party to the resulting sale, and [pending legal confirmation of exact wording] does not currently take compensation from the dealer in connection with any purchase. Submitting an offer in response to a LEVR Auto inquiry constitutes agreement to the terms below for that offer.

## 2. Submitting an Offer — Itemization Requirements

Offers must be complete and itemized, including:
- Vehicle price
- Destination/delivery charge
- Any dealer-installed accessories or packages, **itemized individually** — not bundled into a single "add-on" figure
- Documentation fee
- VIN and, where available, a current photo of the specific unit

## 3. No Undisclosed Fees

Once submitted, an offer's itemized components may not have new fees added at time of purchase. Financing terms (interest rate, term length) naturally vary based on the buyer's approved financing and are not considered part of the base offer — those are expected to differ. Anything else — a fee added because the buyer has no trade-in, a "market adjustment" introduced after the fact, etc. — is not permitted once an itemized offer has been submitted.

## 4. Add-On Removal Requests

Buyers may request that specific dealer-installed add-ons be removed from an offer. The dealer may accept, decline, or counter each request. There's no cap on how many rounds this can go — it continues until resolved.

## 5. Offer Validity, Revocation & Availability Confirmation

Dealer inventory is live and can sell independently of LEVR Auto. If the specific vehicle in an offer sells or otherwise becomes unavailable, the dealer must revoke or update that offer **immediately** through the platform — not on discovery by the buyer. Before a buyer finalizes a purchase, the dealer confirms the unit is still on-site and available.

## 6. Buyer Information & Confidentiality

Prior to an offer being accepted, the dealer receives only the buyer's general region/state (for tax and pricing purposes) and vehicle specifications — no personal identifying information. Buyer contact information is shared only once an offer is accepted and the deal moves to direct dealer-buyer coordination. Dealer agrees not to use any information received through LEVR Auto for marketing or solicitation outside the specific transaction.

## 7. Quality Standards / Termination

LEVR Auto may stop sending inquiries to a dealer that repeatedly violates these terms — undisclosed fees, failure to honor submitted offers, or failure to promptly revoke unavailable offers.
